🏛️ Leadership Update 📅 March 15, 2026

New Executive Leadership Takes Office

CORET Foundation announces transition in leadership with renewed commitment to Humanist values...

The Community of Renaissance Thinkers Foundation (CORET) Nigeria is pleased to announce the appointment of new executive leadership following a successful election held last month. Chief Chukwudi Okafor assumes the role of Community President, bringing over 15 years of experience in civic engagement and social reform.

Dr. Amina Bello joins as Vice President, while Mr. Oluwaseun Adeyemi takes on the role of General Secretary. The new leadership team has outlined an ambitious agenda focused on expanding membership, enhancing digital infrastructure, and amplifying Humanist voices across Nigeria.

Outgoing president Mrs. Grace Okonkwo was celebrated for her tenure, which saw the foundation grow from a small discussion group to a nationally recognized organization with members across all six geopolitical zones.

🤝 Membership Drive 📅 April 1, 2026

Open Call: New and Returning Members Welcome!

CORET Foundation opens membership registration for all Nigerians interested in Humanism, critical thinking, and community development...

CORET Foundation is excited to announce a membership drive open to both new and returning members across Nigeria. Whether you are discovering Humanism for the first time or rejoining our community, we welcome you to be part of Nigeria's fastest-growing secular community.

Members enjoy access to exclusive events, discussion forums, mentorship programs, and our digital Amethyst platform. Benefits include:

  • ✅ Access to the Amethyst digital portal
  • ✅ Voting rights in community decisions
  • ✅ Participation in constitution review
  • ✅ Invitations to regional and national events
  • ✅ Connection with like-minded individuals

To join, simply register through our membership portal or contact our membership team at membership@coret.ng. Returning members can reactivate their accounts by logging into the Amethyst platform.

📜 Governance 📅 April 10, 2026

Constitution Review Committee Launched

CORET Foundation establishes a 7-member committee to review and update the organization's constitution...

The CORET Foundation has officially launched its Constitution Review Committee, tasking a diverse group of seven members with modernizing the organization's governing document. The committee includes legal experts, long-standing members, and representatives from each geopolitical zone.

Key areas of review include:

  • 🔹 Leadership election procedures
  • 🔹 Member rights and responsibilities
  • 🔹 Dispute resolution mechanisms
  • 🔹 Financial transparency guidelines
  • 🔹 Amendment processes

Members are invited to submit feedback through the Amethyst platform. A draft will be presented for community review in June 2026, followed by a final ratification vote. The committee is chaired by legal expert Barrister Adeola Williams, who emphasized the importance of member participation in shaping CORET's future.
