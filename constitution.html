PART I: CHARTER - Sections 1-3 (Name, Vision, Principles)

1. Name & Legal Status: CORET Foundation Nigeria operates as a non-profit, non-partisan civic organization.

2. Vision: A Nigeria built on equity, rational thought, unity, and sustainable development.

3. Core Principles: Equality, human dignity, neutrality, non-discrimination, peaceful dialogue, evidence-based reasoning.

PART I: CHARTER - Sections 4-5 (Human Rights & National Inclusion)

4. Human Rights: Equality and non-discrimination regardless of ethnicity, gender, sexual orientation, or belief system.

5. National Inclusion: Representation from all six geopolitical zones; youth and gender inclusion; linguistic diversity.

PART II: GOVERNANCE - Sections 6-9 (Structure, Membership, Meetings)

6. Governance Structure: National Executive Council (NEC), Standing Committees, decentralized Zonal/State/Local units.

7. Membership: Open to all who accept objectives; categories include General, Active, Volunteer, Executive.

8. Meetings: General, Executive, Committee meetings; quorum of 30% or 50 members.

9. Code of Conduct: Respect, non-discrimination, conflict of interest disclosure.

PART II: GOVERNANCE - Sections 10-18 (Finance, Discipline, Tenure, Amendment)

10. Finance: Donations, grants, dues; dual-signatory system; audit requirements.

11. Discipline: Warnings, suspension, expulsion with due process.

12. Tenure: Two-year terms, renewable once.

13-18: Amendment, dissolution, data protection, risk management, monitoring, adoption.

All sections must have a vote selected before submission.
