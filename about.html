Community of Renaissance Thinkers

Building a Better Nigeria Through Critical Thinking
CORET Foundation Nigeria is a non-profit, non-partisan civic organization dedicated to promoting Humanism, critical thinking, and community development across Nigeria. We bring together thinkers, innovators, and change-makers committed to a better future.
🎯

Our Mission

To foster a community of critical thinkers who champion Humanist values, scientific reasoning, and social progress for a better Nigeria.

👁️

Our Vision

A Nigeria where reason, compassion, and evidence-based thinking guide individual and collective action for the greater good.

🌟 Our Core Values

🔍

Critical Thinking

Questioning assumptions and seeking evidence-based understanding

🤝

Community & Belonging

Building inclusive spaces for like-minded individuals

🧠

Science & Reason

Promoting scientific literacy and rational discourse

⚖️

Creating positive change in our communities
